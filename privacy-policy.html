Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2025

Summary: This is a personal note-taking app for individual use only. All data belongs to and is controlled by the user.

1. Information We Collect

Drop Lite is a personal productivity app that stores:

  • Notes and Entries: Text content, titles, and timestamps of notes you create
  • Device Information: Basic device identifiers for sync purposes
  • Usage Data: Local app usage patterns stored on your device

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Storing and syncing your personal notes
  • Providing offline functionality
  • Maintaining app functionality across your devices

3. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB
  • Cloud Sync: Data is synced to your personal Google Sheets document
  • No Third-Party Access: No data is shared with any third parties

4. Data Security

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
  • API access is protected with personal authentication keys
  • Data is stored in your personal Google account
  • No server-side logging or analytics

5. Your Rights

As the sole user of this personal app, you have complete control:

  • Access: You own all your data
  • Modify: Edit or delete any entries
  • Export: Download data from your Google Sheets
  • Delete: Clear all data from the app and spreadsheet

6. Data Retention

  • Data is retained until you manually delete it
  • Local data can be cleared by clearing browser data
  • Cloud data remains in your Google Sheets until deleted

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in the app and this document. As a personal app, changes are at the discretion of the user/developer.

8. Contact

This is a personal application. For questions about your data, you control all aspects of data handling through your Google account and local device settings.
